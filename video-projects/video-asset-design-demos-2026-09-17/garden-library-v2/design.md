Andy selected: (2) veins physically become the lesson; (3) a continuous camera rise reveals a fixed library arrangement; (5) knowledge is copied without depleting its source. This is a new version; v1 and the parked cutout studies are preserved.

During this beat, the viewer should understand that a useful body of knowledge grows through care and remains available when someone learns from it, by following persistent plant geometry, a rising perspective camera, and a separate lesson copy travelling into the visitor's hand.

## Mechanics
- Six persistent vein paths per leaf are resampled into equal-length point arrays, then interpolated into six lesson strokes. No cross-faded replacement icons. The hero's veins become three graph links and three node rings.
- Fixed 3D plant centers, fixed leaf planes, and fixed root sites define a small garden. A pinhole camera rises from 29 to 80 degrees and pulls back from distance 1450 to 1660. Perspective, leaf foreshortening, projected soil and depth ordering change continuously. Leaves never rearrange in world space. SVG paths are the projected geometry, not a cutaway screenshot or image dissolve. The visitor is a readable camera-facing illustration within this spatial scene.
- At 13.55–14.65 seconds, a new copy lifts from the pink lesson and travels in an arc into the visitor's hand. The source leaf and six diagram paths stay intact. The visitor leaves carrying it, then returns. At 24.8–26.2 seconds they receive a second copy from the reading leaf. Final hold shows that copy beside the original.
