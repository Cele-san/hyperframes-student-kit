# Garden library v1

During this beat, the viewer should understand that thoughtful repeated creation builds a useful collection people revisit, by seeing the same botanical leaves become lessons and the same person visit twice.

Source: Andy's approved garden narration, unchanged. 26 seconds at 1920x1080 / 30 fps. Silent master and a separate captioned timing preview; timing is provisional until the recorded narration exists.

Selected treatment: original OperatorOS V5 glass/editorial background, local Inter/Cormorant/Mono fonts, sharp organic foreground. Original garden shared assets copied unchanged. The botanical form is a custom G04-style topology; the seed-to-leaf growth and leaf rotation are semantic transformations required by Andy's approved concept, rather than decorative movement. Glass remains behind the diagram. Parked cut-paper and stop-motion assets are not used.

Three planted roots, five distinct lesson leaves. The pink seed leads to the pink hero leaf. Veins straighten as lesson icons emerge. Every leaf keeps its same DOM identity while moving into a two-row library; stems recompute to remain attached. No card replacement or cutaway library image. One identifiable visitor, with the same blue backpack, enters, inspects, leaves, returns and explores another lesson. No performance metrics or claims about paid advertising.

Draw state is a pure function of timeline time. No accumulated positions, random state or network dependencies. Each frame recomputes the entire SVG from time, including backward seeking. SVG illustrations are authored diagrams, not generated or captured evidence.
