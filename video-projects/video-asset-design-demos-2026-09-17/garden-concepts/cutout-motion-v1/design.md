# Cutout motion test v1

14-second silent proof of the selected leaf-to-library concept. Not the full narrated garden sequence. Uses generated alpha plant cutouts, an independently moving clipped cover leaf, and the approved concept library plate separated into spatial masks. 30 fps delivery with approximately 10–12 pose changes per second during actions.

0–1.8: closed plant holds and settles. 1.8–3.1: cover folds away. 3.1–5: lesson hold. 5–7: plant moves to upper-right library position. 7–9.3: four companion lessons appear in sequence. 9.15–10.65: connecting roots settle. 10.65–14: library hold.

This proof uses a matched cut from the moving puppet to the overhead illustrated leaf at 6.8 seconds. It does not claim a physically continuous 3D camera orbit. Library diagrams remain symbolic placeholders. Full narration, care actions and visitor return are outside this bounded motion test.
